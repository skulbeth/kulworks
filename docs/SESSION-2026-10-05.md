## 2. Where they live, and what the card shows

**Placement, Sam's call:** the `/testimonials` page **only**. The home-page strip
was removed and the page is linked from the **footer** under Explore. That also
closed a gap — the page was in the sitemap with nothing linking to it except the
home strip, and only once there were more than three published. `Testimonials.tsx`
is kept but unrendered, the same convention as `ComingSoon.tsx`.

**The card went through three shapes before it was right**, and the lesson is the
same each time: *pictures do not belong inline on a card in a grid.*

1. Screenshot inline — "that looks terrible". A chat window at card width is
   unreadable, and because the shots are all different shapes the grid came out
   ragged with tall empty gaps.
2. Screenshot behind a link, item photo inline — still wrong, same reason.
3. **Both as plain text links** under the quote, "See the item" and "See the
   message", opening in the existing `Lightbox` (`TestimonialProof.tsx`). Cards
   stay tight, the words lead.

A testimonial with a picture but **no typed words** still shows its image inline,
since otherwise there would be nothing to read.

Also: the quote had `flex-1`, which stretched it and pushed the links to the bottom
of the card away from the words they belong to. Quote and links now sit in one
block at the top and the slack falls below.

## 3. Two bugs worth remembering

**The image route caches hard per id.** Its own comment said "content for an id
never changes", and replacing a screenshot broke that assumption — the corrected
file was live but browsers kept serving the old one for an hour. This is why Sam
kept seeing his un-blurred name after it had been fixed. URLs now carry
`?v=<updatedAt>`. If you ever replace a stored image, change the URL.

**An in-app `<Link>` does not set `document.referrer`.** The Who It's For back
button tried to name the page you came from and always said plain "Back". The two
links that point there now pass `?from=services|portfolio`; the referrer is only a
fallback for a real page load, such as arriving from search.

## 4. Item photos

`Testimonial.photoPath` was added alongside `imagePath` (migration
`20261005140000_testimonial_item_photo`, additive, applied to production). One
route serves both behind the same published-and-consented gate, with `?kind=item`

old object is deleted rather than orphaned.

## 6. Facebook link previews cache hard

Sam reported the share preview was "still not fixed" long after the OG image had
been corrected. **The site was serving the right thing the whole time** — verified
by requesting the page as `facebookexternalhit/1.1` and downloading
`og-default.png` to look at it.

Facebook keeps its scraped copy until told otherwise, and nothing site-side can
force a refresh. The fix is Sam opening
**developers.facebook.com/tools/debug/**, pasting the URL and clicking **Scrape
Again** (needs his login). Confirmed fixed that way.

**Check the live OG tags before assuming a site bug.**
