## Super rush replaces the automatic rush fee

Before: $15 added whenever the due date was under 3 days out, which charged ordinary ~2-day
jobs. Now `SUPER_RUSH_FEE = 25`, opt-in: "Super rush: place my project above others in the
queue". A due date under 2 days (`isTightDate`) only shows a nudge. Checkbox in the
calculator and the quote form, `&rush=1` handoff, saved as `Submission.superRush`, SUPER RUSH
badge in admin submissions. Invoices still take it as a manual line.
