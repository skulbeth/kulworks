# Session log, 2026-10-22

From an outside review of the site (Sam pasted it in). Sam picked: a real photo in the
hero, the footer Cloudflare box, bigger service photos ("not too big"), the factory pitch
higher up, consistent I/we, and dropping the 9% service charge.

- **Hero photo:** the PANIC deck fanned at an angle (`portfolio/cards/panic-cards-spread-still.webp`,
  1600x1067). Picked from every image on the site against the review's brief (cards as the
  subject, three-quarter angle, landscape, fills the frame, finished product, already
  published). The other card shots are flat-on or top-down. Two columns from `lg`, under the
  buttons on phones; eager + `fetchPriority="high"`. h1 steps down to 5xl at `lg` so the
  narrower column doesn't wrap into five lines. Sam may swap in a purpose-shot photo later:
  just change the `src`.
- **"Between a home printer and a factory"** (with the See the Work card) now sits right
  under the hero instead of fourth.
- **Service cards:** a 5:2 photo banner instead of a 56px icon. New optional `cardImage` on
  `Service` (card printing uses `cards-variety.webp`; its `iconImage` is a small portrait
  that crops badly). The Services page keeps using `iconImage` as before.
- **Footer white box:** Turnstile now uses `appearance: "interaction-only"`, so it only shows
  when Cloudflare actually wants a click (it showed "Success!" in a white box under the
  newsletter form, on every page).
- **I/we:** "we" for the shop (quote CTAs, the contact page, the form placeholder), "I" kept
  where it's Sam personally ("Catch me at a convention", "See my models on MakerWorld").
- **9% service charge removed:** `site.payments.defaultServiceCharge` is now 0, so new
  invoices start at 0% (the editor's toggle still adds a charge for a one-off). The
  calculator shows one total, no service-charge rows; the form note, the cost guide and the
  terms no longer mention it. Existing invoices keep the rate they were saved with.
  Calculator: 54 print-ready cards = $38.50.

Smoke test 20/21 (the old demo-data check). Build clean. Dev "1 issue" badge is React's
dev-only eval warning under the site CSP, not from these changes.
